Family photos

Transfer "Family photos" to Ana Lima?

Step 3 of 3 · Confirm
  • Ana Lima becomes the owner of every photo and video in this library, with their tags, and any albums or stacks that contain only these photos.
  • Ana Lima gets copies of the people and pets in these photos, with their names and birthdays. Hidden and favorite settings are not copied. Before you confirm, you can merge each one into a person Ana Lima already has. Jaime keeps their own people and the photos of those people in their other libraries.
  • A shared space owned by Ana Lima is created so Jaime can keep viewing these photos, read-only. Jaime's favorites stay with Jaime. They lose access when they leave that space or the space is deleted.
  • Jaime may keep seeing these photos as their own in the official Immich app until they reinstall it. Gallery apps update on their own.
  • Albums and stacks that also contain photos outside this library stay with Jaime. The transferred photos are removed from them.
  • The library cannot be scanned, and its photos cannot be edited, until the transfer finishes. If the transfer is interrupted, it resumes. If it fails, the library stays with Jaime.
  • When it finishes, you get a report of what moved, what was removed from mixed albums and stacks, and which people were merged or copied.
The transfer runs in the background
